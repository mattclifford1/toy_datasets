Hepatocellular Carcinoma (HCC) survival dataset.

Binary classification: predict 1-year survival of HCC patients — lives (class 1)
or dies (class 0). Collected at a University Hospital in Portugal for 165 real
patients, with 49 features (23 quantitative, 26 qualitative) selected per the
EASL-EORTC clinical guidelines: demographics, risk factors, comorbidities and
laboratory measurements.

A heterogeneous, partially missing dataset (~10% of values missing, sentinel
'?'), with mild class imbalance (102 lives vs 63 dies). Missing values are
median-imputed during loading.

Citation: Santos et al., "A new cluster-based oversampling method for improving
survival prediction of hepatocellular carcinoma patients", Journal of Biomedical
Informatics, 58, 49-59, 2015.
Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/423/hcc+survival
Stats: 165 samples, 49 features.
