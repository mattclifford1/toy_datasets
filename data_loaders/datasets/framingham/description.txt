Framingham Heart Study — 10-year coronary heart disease (CHD) risk.

Binary classification: predict whether a patient develops coronary heart disease
within 10 years (class 1) or not (class 0) from demographic, behavioural and
medical risk factors (age, smoking, blood pressure, cholesterol, BMI, glucose,
diabetes, etc.). The classes are imbalanced (~15% positive) and several columns
contain missing values, which are median-imputed during loading.

Derived from the Framingham Heart Study longitudinal cohort, widely mirrored as
the Kaggle "framingham.csv".
Stats: 4240 samples, 15 features.
