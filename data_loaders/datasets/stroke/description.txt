Stroke Prediction dataset.

Binary classification: predict whether a patient has had a stroke (class 1) or
not (class 0) from demographic and clinical attributes (age, hypertension, heart
disease, average glucose level, BMI, smoking status, work/residence type, etc.).
Strongly imbalanced — only ~5% of patients are positive — and the BMI column has
missing values, making this a good stress test for class-balancing and missing
data handling.

Categorical columns (gender, ever_married, work_type, residence, smoking_status)
are integer-encoded and missing BMI values are median-imputed during loading.

Source: Kaggle "Stroke Prediction Dataset" (fedesoriano), id column removed.
Stats: 5110 samples, 10 features.
