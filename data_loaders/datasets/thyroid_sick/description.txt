Thyroid Disease (sick) dataset.

Binary classification: predict whether a patient is sick (referred for thyroid
dysfunction, class 1) or negative (class 0) from demographic details, clinical
flags and thyroid hormone assays (TSH, T3, TT4, T4U, FTI). A classic highly
imbalanced / anomaly-detection benchmark: roughly 6% of records are positive.

Categorical t/f flags are encoded to 1/0, sex to Female=0/Male=1, the referral
source is integer-encoded, missing values (sentinel '?') are median-imputed and
the near-empty TBG assay column is dropped. The per-record id suffix on the
class label is removed during loading.

Source: https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/102/thyroid+disease
Files: thyroid-disease/sick.data and sick.names (Garavan Institute, Sydney).
Stats: 2800 samples, 28 features after dropping TBG.
